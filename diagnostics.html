The biomarkers we track & optimize.

Apolipoprotein B (ApoB)

Vascular Health & Cardio

Measures the precise particle count of plaque-causing lipoproteins. ApoB is the ultimate medical predictor for cardiovascular safety, far outperforming standard cholesterol tests.

GlycA (Chronic Inflammation)

Cellular Health & Immune

A cutting-edge biomarker that aggregates systemic inflammatory loads. Spotting micro-inflammation early is vital to prevent rapid cellular senescence and tissue decay.

Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)

Metabolic Wellness & Glucose

Measures three-month cellular glucose averages, establishing your clinical metabolic baseline to optimize insulin sensitivity and metabolic rates.

DNA Methylation (Epigenetics)

Biological Age & Lifespan

Analyzes chemical tags on your genome to evaluate your biological age. Helps confirm whether your cellular age is outperforming your chronological age.

hs-CRP (High-Sensitivity CRP)

Vascular Stress & Recovery

Monitors localized cardiovascular stress and inflammation. Helps clinicians titrate metabolic plans to improve cellular recovery.

Hormonal Pools (Free & Total)

Endocrine & Vitality

Audits bio-available hormones essential to maintain cognitive acuity, cellular metabolism, skeletal muscle volume, and general daily vitality.
