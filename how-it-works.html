Your Process

From intake to long-term results.

01

Choose your treatment focus

Start by selecting the care path that fits your goals — metabolic health, longevity optimization, hormone support, or personalized wellness care.

02

Complete intake and secure checkout

Fill out your medical history and submit your treatment request through a secure, HIPAA-aligned flow. This helps your provider assess fit safely.

03

Provider review and plan approval

A licensed North Star MD provider reviews your history, labs, and goals. If needed, they follow up with recommendations before approval.

04

Receive medication and onboarding guidance

Once approved, your medication is prepared and delivered to your door with clear instructions and onboarding support from day one.

05

Optimize with ongoing physician support

Your plan evolves as your body and goals evolve. Expect regular check-ins, progress tracking, and data-informed adjustments for lasting outcomes.

FAQs

Questions, answered clearly.

How quickly can I get started?

Most patients complete intake in minutes, and clinical review is often completed within 24 hours.

Do I need a live video appointment?

Not always. Many care paths are reviewed asynchronously. If your provider needs additional context, they'll reach out directly.

Is support included after treatment starts?

Yes. Ongoing guidance, check-ins, and treatment adjustments are core to the North Star MD experience.
