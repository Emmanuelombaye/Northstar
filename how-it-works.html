FAQs

How quickly can I get started?

Most patients complete intake in minutes, and clinical review is often completed within 24 hours.

Do I need a live video appointment?

Not always. Many care paths are reviewed asynchronously. If your provider needs additional context, they'll reach out directly.

Is support included after treatment starts?

Yes. Ongoing guidance, check-ins, and treatment adjustments are core to the North Star MD experience.
